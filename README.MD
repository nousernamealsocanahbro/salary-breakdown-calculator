# Salary breakdown calculator
A calculator that takes your salary and deducts it from CPF and savings to see how much spendings you'll have.

# Usage
This calculator assumes you are a Singaporean citizen or a 3rd year onwards PR that's under 55 years old. 

To use it, simply type in your salary and the percentage of your salary you want to save. It will do the maths for you and then print out how much of your salary will go to CPF contributions and saving and how much you'll have left for spendings.
