# Salary breakdown calculator
A program that calculates how much of your salary is left after CPF and savings. 

## Assumptions

Program assumes you are under 55, are a Singaporean Citizen or have been PR for at least 3 years, and want to save 20% of your salary after CPF.

## Sources
Calculations are based off of: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/employer-obligations/how-much-cpf-contributions-to-pay. Rates are based off of January 2026.
